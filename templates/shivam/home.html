INTRODUCTION

Shivam Construction Company is engaged in large format earthworks, cross-country piping, irrigation works, industrial plant buildings, commercial and IT spaces, plant fabrication and erection (especially in power, petrochemical, and pharmaceutical areas).

We have expertise in Civil activities, including Construction of Industrial and Residential Buildings, heavy equipment foundations, and Cooling Towers.

Our Motto: To exceed the customer expectations by understanding their needs and delivering them with specified quality, within the agreed time and thus becoming their trusted partners.

We believe the workplace is where one should unleash creative excellence, possible through "unbounded freedom bounded only by one's own conscience."
